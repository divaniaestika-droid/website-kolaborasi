How Old Computer Parts Are Transformed into a Decorative Clock

 Electronic waste is one of the environmental problems caused by the rapid development of technology. Old computer mouse and CDs are often discarded when they are damaged or no longer needed. However, these materials can still be reused to create useful products. One creative way to recycle electronic waste is by transforming an old computer mouse and a CD into a decorative clock with decorative lights. This transformation shows how unused electronic materials can become valuable and functional products.

 The transformation begins when an unused computer mouse and a CD are collected. The mouse is cleaned and prepared to be used as the base or stand of the clock. Meanwhile, the CD is cleaned and used as the clock face. A clock mechanism is then installed in the center of the CD, and the clock hands are attached. After that, decorative lights are placed inside the computer mouse to create an attractive lighting effect.

 After preparing the main materials, the CD is attached to the computer mouse to form the clock structure. Numbers from one to twelve are arranged around the edge of the CD to show the time clearly. The clock hands are placed in the center and connected to the clock mechanism. The decorative lights inside the mouse are arranged neatly to make the product look more attractive. Finally, the mouse is positioned underneath the CD to support the clock and keep it standing. Once all the components are properly attached, the materials become a complete decorative clock.

 As a result, an old computer mouse and a CD are transformed into a unique decorative clock with additional lighting. The product can be used to show the time while also serving as a creative decoration for a room or desk. The decorative lights make the clock more attractive, especially in low-light conditions. This transformation helps reduce electronic waste and encourages people to reuse old materials. Therefore, turning unused computer components into decorative products is one way to support a more sustainable environment.

How to turn used electronic waste into a multifunctional clock

 Electronic waste is one of the environmental problems caused by the increasing use of technology. Old computer parts, such as CDs and computer mice, are often thrown away when they are no longer used. However, these materials can still be reused to create useful products. One creative way to recycle electronic waste is by transforming old computer components into an analog clock.

 The transformation begins by preparing the used materials, such as an old CD, a computer mouse, and other supporting materials. The CD is cleaned and used as the main clock surface. Meanwhile, the old computer mouse is used as the clock stand. The numbers are made from paper or cardboard and arranged around the CD. After that, the clock machine and its hands are installed in the center.

 The materials are combined to form a functional analog clock. The CD becomes the clock face, while the computer mouse provides support to keep the clock standing. The numbers are attached around the edges of the CD to show the time clearly. Finally, the clock hands and other components are assembled to complete the product.

 As a result, unused computer components are transformed into a unique and functional analog clock. This product can be used to show the time while also serving as a decorative item. By reusing old electronic materials, this project helps reduce electronic waste and encourages creativity. Therefore, transforming used computer parts into useful products is a simple way to support the 3R principle (Reduce, Reuse, and Recycle) and protect the environment.

How Used Computer Parts Are Transformed into an Analog Clock

Unused computer parts, such as an unused computer mouse, an unused keyboard, an unused CD, and other unused materials, are often thrown away. However, these materials can still be reused to create useful and creative products. One example is transforming an unused CD, an unused keyboard, and an unused computer mouse into a unique analog clock.

 The process begins by preparing an unused mouse, an unused keyboard, an unused CD, a PVC pipe, a decorative light for the mouse, clock hands, a hot glue gun, a small screwdriver, a battery, and a CD as a support. First, these materials are cleaned and prepared. The unused CD is selected as the main part of the clock surface, while the computer mouse is prepared to direct light inside the mouse and serves as a decorative lamp.

 Next, the unused CD is placed inside a circular frame to form the clock face. The numbers from one to twelve are arranged around the CD to show the time. After that, the clock mechanism is installed in the center of the CD, and the arrow-shaped clock hands are attached. Then, the unused keyboard keys are arranged to form the words "X SATA". After that, the PVC pipe is installed between the top and the bottom and joined with the mouse, with the mouse as the bottom support. Finally, the materials are joined carefully using hot glue to make the clock stable and neat.

 a result, a used CD and an old computer mouse are transformed into a unique analog clock. The clock can be used to show the time and decorate a room. This creative product gives unused materials a new purpose and helps reduce electronic waste.
